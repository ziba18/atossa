## New regressions and bugs found

1. **Strength value corruption (high).** In the noted card, typing "6 usually, 9 at worst" into How strong saves **10/10 at worst**. This wrong value flows to the Latest note, the Timeline chart and the GP summary.
2. **Unanswered = 0 (high).** Notes saved without a strength (Save it from the confirm card, or "That's all for now") are stored and shown as **0/10**. They create 0 bars on the chart, make the doctor page say "0 to 10 out of 10", and pre-select 0 in the editor.
3. **Chip row can't scroll (high).** "Just a quick note" can't be reached at any phone width, and at 320 px neither can "Something new".
4. **Toast covers sheet controls (medium).** The toast sits over the escape chips, the follow-up input, "Looks right, save" and the Flow buttons. Several toasts stack. Swipe to dismiss did not work.
5. **Note counts disagree (medium).** After one save, the toast said "13 notes ready for your GP" while the appointment card said "15 notes ready" and the doctor page said "13 entries". Earlier, the toast said 9 while the card said 11.
6. **"Not sure" listed as something that stopped Sam (medium).** On the doctor page, "What stopped me doing things" has an entry that just says "Not sure", and it is counted in the (8).
7. **New filler text (medium).** "When: Mentioned in my words" on the noted card becomes "How long: Mentioned in my words" on the GP summary. This is the same problem as the old "In your own words".
8. **Log bleeding defaults (medium).** "Started today" and Flow "None" are pre-selected (a contradiction). The options have no aria state. A period logged today doesn't change the doctor page's "Latest period" (still 21–26 Aug).
9. **Doctor "Most recent appointment" is wrong (low).** It shows the March 2020 appointment although there is one on Thu 24 Sep.
10. **Keep-this-note dead end (low).** There is no way back to the note from the confirm card, only Save it / Discard.
11. **Rough day Latest note shows the value twice (low).** "Rough day · 7/10" and then a fact tag "7/10".
12. **Small copy and polish issues (low).** The follow-up progress reads "1 of up to 1". The Type tab doesn't autofocus the textarea. "When opening bowels" is offered as a *Where* option. The appointment year picker can't go past 2026.

## Overall score: **7 / 10** (was 5 / 10)

Most of the design system now matches spec: tokens, radii, type on the main screens, serif rules, selection, tab bar, cards and chevrons. The capture flow is much better: the mic records on the first tap, X and Escape now close the sheet, follow-ups ask only about missing fields and support multi-select, and Rough day no longer puts words in Sam's mouth. The score stays at 7 because of two data-integrity bugs that put wrong numbers in the GP summary (10/10 from a typed "6 / 9", and 0/10 for unanswered), and because one starter chip can't be reached and the toast covers sheet buttons.
