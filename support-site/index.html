Frequently asked questions

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Resetting a password by email isn't available in the app yet. Email zamaniziba18@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we'll help you get back in. If you signed up with Apple or Google, use that button again on the sign-in screen.

Sign in with Apple or Google isn't working. What should I do?

Make sure you're on the latest version of Atossa from the App Store and signed in to iCloud (for Apple) on your device. If it still doesn't work, email us so we can look into it.

Does Atossa diagnose conditions?

No. Atossa doesn't diagnose, name conditions or give medical advice. It helps you describe your symptoms clearly so you and your doctor can decide what to do next. If you feel very unwell, contact your doctor or emergency services.

Why doesn't voice input work?

Voice input uses Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone. Allow microphone and speech recognition access when asked, or turn them on in Settings → Atossa. Your speech is turned into text on your phone; no audio is sent anywhere.

How do I share my summary with my doctor?

Open For my doctor, choose what to include, then tap Share PDF, print it, or show it on screen during your appointment.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, tap your avatar (top right), then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything you've saved. For a copy of your data, email zamaniziba18@gmail.com from the address tied to your account.

What devices does Atossa support?

Atossa runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 15.1 or later.

Privacy

Reporting a bug

Found something broken? Send us an email with:

  • The device and iOS version you're using (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 18.2).
  • The Atossa version, found in Profile → Settings.
  • A short description of what you did and what went wrong. A screenshot helps a lot.
